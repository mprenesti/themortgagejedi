One of the biggest sources of stress in buying a home is not knowing what comes next. You send in your documents, and then you just wait and wonder. Is it moving? Did something go wrong? Why is it so quiet?

Let me take that fear away. When you know the timeline, the whole thing feels calm instead of chaotic. So here is what actually happens, week by week, from the moment you start to the day you get your keys in Las Vegas.

Every deal is a little different, but most purchases wrap up in about 21 to 30 days once you are under contract. Here is how those weeks usually go.

## Before You Shop: The Pre-Approval

This is the step people skip, and it costs them. Before you tour a single home, get pre-approved.

Here is what happens in this stage:

- We look at your income, your credit, and your debts.
- We figure out a comfortable monthly payment, not just the biggest number a bank will hand you.
- I shop your file across multiple wholesale lenders to find your best fit.
- You get a pre-approval letter that makes your offers strong.

This part can happen fast, sometimes in a day or two. And it sets the tone for everything. A strong pre-approval means sellers take you seriously in a competitive Henderson market.

## Week 1: Under Contract and Application

You found the home. Your offer got accepted. Congratulations, the clock starts now.

In this first week:

- We lock in your full application.
- You give a good faith deposit, usually held in escrow.
- We open the loan and order the paperwork.
- I go over your locked rate and your expected payment so there are no surprises.

This is also when we talk about your rate lock and protect your payment. The pace picks up here, and that is a good thing. Movement means progress.

## Week 1 to 2: Appraisal and Processing

Now the loan is in motion behind the scenes. Two big things happen.

First, the appraisal. The lender orders a professional to confirm the home is worth what you are paying. This protects you and the lender both. You do not want to overpay, and neither do we.

Second, processing. My team gathers and organizes everything. This is when we may ask you for a few more documents. Please send them fast. The number one thing that slows a loan down is waiting on paperwork from the buyer. Quick responses keep your closing on schedule.

## Week 2 to 3: Underwriting

This is the heart of the process. The underwriter is the person who reviews your entire file and gives the final green light.

They check:

- Your income and job.
- Your assets and down payment.
- Your credit.
- The appraisal.
- That everything matches and makes sense.

Sometimes the underwriter asks for one or two more items. That is normal. It does not mean anything is wrong. It just means they are being thorough. We handle these requests quickly and keep you moving toward the finish line.

During this stretch, do not make any big money moves. Do not buy a car. Do not open a new credit card. Do not change jobs without telling me. Keep everything steady until you close.

## Week 3 to 4: Clear to Close and Closing Day

This is the moment you have been waiting for. Clear to close. It means the underwriter approved everything and you are ready to sign.

Here is how the finish looks:

- You get your final numbers, including your exact cash to close.
- You do a final walkthrough of the home.
- You sign your closing documents.
- The loan funds and records.
- You get your keys.

That last part never gets old for me. Handing a client the keys to their home in Las Vegas is the whole reason I do this.

## How to Keep Your Timeline on Track

Want the smoothest possible ride? Here is your job as the buyer.

- Respond to document requests fast.
- Keep your finances steady and boring until closing.
- Do not open new credit or make big purchases.
- Ask me questions any time something is unclear.

Do those four things and your loan tends to fly. I will handle the rest and keep you updated so you are never left wondering.
