You know the feeling. A new buyer lead comes in, they sound excited, and you block off your Saturday to show them homes. Then you find out halfway through the day that their credit is not ready or their budget is nowhere near the homes they want to see. That is a hard hit to your time and your energy.

I work with a lot of agents around Las Vegas, and I want to help you fix this. Vetting a buyer lead before the first showing is not rude. It is professional. Here is how to do it without scaring anyone off.

## Why Vetting Early Protects Everyone

When you qualify a lead up front, you protect three people.

- You protect yourself from burning weekends on buyers who cannot buy.
- You protect the buyer from falling in love with a home they cannot get.
- You protect the deal so it does not fall apart at the worst moment.

A buyer who is truly ready will respect that you take this seriously. The ones who push back on a simple pre-approval step are often the ones who were not ready anyway. Better to know now.

## The Questions to Ask on the First Call

You do not need to run credit yourself. You just need to ask the right things and then hand them to a lender who will. Try these:

- Have you spoken with a lender or a broker yet?
- Do you have a price range or a monthly payment in mind?
- Are you planning to use any down payment assistance?
- Is there anything on your credit we should know about early?

Notice that middle question. A monthly payment in mind is gold. Buyers who think in payments are usually more grounded than buyers who only think in purchase price. That mindset makes for a smoother deal.

## Make Pre-Approval the First Step, Not the Last

The single best filter is a real pre-approval. Not a rate quote, not a soft guess. An actual pre-approval where a broker has looked at income, credit, and assets.

Here is the line I suggest agents use. Tell the buyer, before we spend a Saturday looking at homes, let us get you pre-approved so we know exactly what fits your payment. That way when you find the one, you can move fast in a competitive Las Vegas market.

That framing does two things. It sounds like you are helping them, because you are. And it quietly filters out the folks who are not ready.

This is where I come in. When you send me a lead, I look at their real numbers and I lead with the payment. My whole approach is finding a comfortable monthly payment, not the biggest approval. That means the buyers I pre-approve tend to buy homes they can actually keep.

## A Simple System You Can Copy

Here is the flow I suggest for every new buyer lead:

1. Quick call to ask the four questions above.
2. Send them straight to a broker for a real pre-approval.
3. Get the payment range back so you show the right homes.
4. Book the showing with a buyer who is truly ready.

Do this and your Saturdays start converting. Your close rate goes up and your stress goes down.
