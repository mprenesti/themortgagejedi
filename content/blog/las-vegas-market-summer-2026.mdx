Summer is here, and the Las Vegas market always shifts a little when the heat rolls in. Families want to be settled before school starts. Buyers who waited out the spring start to make moves. I like to do a check in around this time of year so my clients know what to expect. Here is what the numbers are telling me heading into summer 2026, and more importantly, what it means for you.

## The general trends I am watching

Before I list anything, one quick note. These are broad context numbers meant to help you understand the direction of the market. They are not guarantees, and they do not tell you what your specific home or loan will look like. Every deal is its own story.

With that said, here is the general read for Las Vegas and Henderson.

- Inventory is healthier than it has been in a while. More homes on the market means more choices and a little less pressure.
- Time on market is stretching out for a lot of listings. Sellers who priced high in the spring are adjusting.
- Seller concessions are common. That can mean closing cost help or a credit you can use to buy down your rate.
- Rates are still moving week to week. We plan around your budget instead of trying to guess the perfect day.

None of this is a reason to panic or to rush. It is a reason to be prepared.

## Why summer can work in your favor

A lot of people think spring is the only time to buy. I disagree. Summer in Las Vegas has some quiet advantages for a ready buyer.

Some sellers who listed in the spring are now motivated. Their home did not sell as fast as they hoped, and they are ready to make a deal. That is where a prepared buyer with an approval in hand can do very well.

There is also less competition from buyers who give up when the temperature climbs. If you are willing to look at homes in July, you may find sellers paying more attention to your offer. Fewer bidding wars can mean a better price and better terms.

## Keep your eye on the payment

You have heard me say it before and I will say it again. Shop the payment, not the price tag. When rates move around like they are now, the payment is the only number that really tells you if a home fits your life.

Here is how I keep my Las Vegas clients grounded.

- We set a comfortable monthly number first, then find the price range that fits it.
- We run a few scenarios so you can feel the difference between homes at different prices.
- We use seller concessions wisely. Sometimes a rate buydown lowers your payment more than a price cut would.
- We keep reserves in mind so a hot Nevada summer utility bill does not catch you off guard.

A comfortable payment is the whole point. The biggest approval you can get is not the goal.
