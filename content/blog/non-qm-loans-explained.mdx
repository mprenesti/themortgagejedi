## What Non-QM Really Means

QM stands for Qualified Mortgage. Those are the loans that follow the strict government backed rulebook created after the last housing crash. They work great for people with steady W2 jobs and clean, simple finances.

Non-QM simply means the loan does not follow that exact rulebook. It does not mean it is bad or dangerous. It means the lender has more room to look at your full picture instead of forcing you into one narrow set of rules.

These loans still check that you can repay. In fact, that is the whole point. They just measure your ability to pay in a way that actually fits your life.

## Who Non-QM Loans Are Built For

The traditional system leaves a lot of good borrowers behind. Non-QM fills that gap. Here in Las Vegas and Henderson, I use these loans for people like:

- Self-employed business owners whose tax returns hide their real income
- Real estate investors who want the property to qualify on its own rent
- Retirees with big assets but little monthly income on paper
- Folks who just recovered from a bankruptcy or a rough patch
- Foreign nationals buying a home or investment property
- Gig workers and freelancers with strong but nontraditional income

If any of that sounds like you, a no from a big bank does not mean you cannot buy. It just means you were talking to the wrong lender.

## The Main Types of Non-QM Loans

Non-QM is really a family of loans, not one single product. Here are the ones I use most often:

- Bank statement loans, which use your deposits to prove income instead of tax returns
- P&L loans, which qualify you off a profit and loss statement
- DSCR loans, which let a rental property qualify based on the rent it brings in
- Asset based loans, which use your savings and investments as your income
- Interest only options for buyers who want more payment flexibility

Each one solves a different problem. My job is to match the right tool to your situation.

## Yes, the Rate Is Usually a Little Higher

I always shoot straight with people, so here it is. Non-QM loans often carry a slightly higher rate than a standard loan. That is the trade off for the flexibility.

But here is what I tell every client. A slightly higher rate on a home you can actually buy beats a great rate on a home you cannot. And many folks refinance into a lower rate down the road once their situation lines up with a traditional loan. A Non-QM loan can be the bridge that gets you in the door now.
