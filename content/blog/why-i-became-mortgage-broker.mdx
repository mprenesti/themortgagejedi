People ask me all the time how I ended up doing this work. The short version is that I know what it feels like to be on the other side of the desk. I was a renter who dreamed about owning, and the road to my own front door shaped how I do business today. Let me tell you the story.

## Life as a renter

For years I rented. I paid on time every month, and I watched that money go to a landlord's mortgage instead of my own. I remember doing the math and feeling frustrated. I was building someone else's wealth while my own stayed flat.

I wanted to own a home. But the process felt like a locked door. The paperwork was confusing. The numbers felt scary. And I did not feel like the people I talked to were really on my side. It seemed like everyone wanted to push me toward the biggest loan I could get, not the one that made sense for my life.

That feeling stuck with me. I did not want to feel small or confused about one of the biggest decisions a person makes. I promised myself that if I ever got into this business, I would do it differently.

## The day I got my keys

When I finally bought my own place, everything changed. I still remember standing in the empty living room, keys in hand, and feeling like I had finally arrived. It was mine. Nobody could raise my rent or tell me to move.

But the thing I remember most was not the house itself. It was the relief. The stress of the process was over, and I realized how much better it could have been if someone had just walked me through it in plain language. If someone had focused on what I could comfortably afford instead of the maximum I could borrow.

That is the moment the idea took hold. I wanted to be the person I wish I had when I was a nervous first time buyer.

## Why I chose to be a broker

Once I decided to get into mortgages, I had a choice. I could work for one bank and sell one menu of products. Or I could become a broker and shop for my clients across many options. It was an easy call.

As a broker, I am on your side of the table. I take your file to multiple wholesale lenders and let them compete for your business. That means I can find the program and pricing that actually fit you, instead of forcing you into whatever one bank happens to offer.

Here is what that lets me do for the people I work with in Las Vegas and Henderson.

- Compare real options side by side, so you can see the true cost.
- Match you with a lender that fits your specific situation, whether you are self employed or a first time buyer.
- Find creative solutions when your file is not a perfect square.
- Keep the focus on a payment you can live with, not the biggest number you can chase.

## The payment comes first, always

Because of my own story, I built my whole approach around the monthly payment. A big approval number does not put food on the table or let you sleep at night. A comfortable payment does.

When I sit down with a client, I do not start with how much house you can get. I start with what you want to spend each month and still feel good. Then we work backward to a price that fits. That way you are never stretched thin, and you actually enjoy owning your home instead of dreading the payment.

I learned that lesson as a renter looking in from the outside. I do not want anyone I work with to feel the way I felt back then. Confused, pressured, and unsure who was really on their side.

## This is personal for me

Helping people go from renting to owning is not just a job for me. It is personal. Every time I hand a client the news that they are approved, I remember standing in my own empty living room with the keys in my hand. I get to help other people feel that, and there is nothing better.
