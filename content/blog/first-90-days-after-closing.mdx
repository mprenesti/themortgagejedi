Closing day feels like the finish line. You sign a stack of papers, you get the keys, and you finally walk into your own place. Here is the truth. Closing is really the starting line.

The first 90 days set the tone for how smooth your homeownership feels. Most people never get a plan for this part. They just wing it. Let me walk you through it the way I would with a friend here in Las Vegas.

## Week One: Handle the Basics

The first week is about getting settled and getting protected.

- Change the locks or rekey them. You do not know who has copies.
- Set up your utilities in your name. Power, water, gas, internet, trash.
- Save your closing documents in one folder. Digital and paper both.
- Find your main water shutoff valve. You want to know this before you need it.

Take a slow walk through the house with fresh eyes. Note anything that needs attention. A slow drain. A loose railing. A vent that needs a filter. Little stuff now saves you bigger stuff later.

## Weeks Two to Four: Get Your Money Set Up

This is where the payment-first mindset really pays off. My whole approach is built around a monthly payment you are comfortable with, not the biggest loan a lender will hand you. So protect that comfort.

- Confirm how you pay your mortgage. Set up autopay if it fits your style.
- Know your exact due date and your grace period.
- Build a small home fund. Even a little each month adds up for repairs.
- Keep receipts for anything you fix or improve. They can matter later.

If your loan has an escrow account for taxes and insurance, understand what is inside your payment. Part goes to the loan. Part goes to those bills. When you know the pieces, nothing surprises you.

## Watch for the Mortgage Transfer Notice

Here is something a lot of new homeowners in Henderson and across Nevada do not expect. Your loan can get sold or transferred to a new servicer. That is normal. It does not change your rate or your terms.

You will get a letter telling you where to send your payment. Then you get a second letter from the new company. If you ever get just one letter and something feels off, slow down and verify. Scammers love this moment. Call the number on your official statement, not a number from a random letter.

## Weeks Five to Twelve: Settle In and Plan Ahead

By now the boxes are unpacked and life feels normal. This is a good time to think a little further out.

- Review your homeowners insurance. Make sure the coverage still fits.
- Start a simple maintenance calendar. Filters, smoke detectors, the water heater.
- Watch your first full statement to confirm everything looks right.
- Keep an eye on rates. If they drop, a future refinance might help your payment.

You do not need to obsess over any of this. You just need a light plan so you stay ahead instead of reacting.
