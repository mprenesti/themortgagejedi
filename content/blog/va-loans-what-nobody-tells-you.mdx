If you served, you earned one of the strongest home loan benefits out there. The VA loan is a real gift. But a lot of veterans in Las Vegas only hear the headline. Zero down. That is great, and it is true, but it is only part of the story.

Let me fill in the parts that often get skipped. These are the details that help you use the benefit the right way.

## Zero Down Is Real, But Understand Why

Yes, you can buy with no down payment. That is the piece everyone knows. What people miss is why that matters for your payment.

With most loans, a low down payment means monthly mortgage insurance. The VA loan does not require that. So you get zero down and no monthly mortgage insurance. That combination keeps your payment lower than most other options.

That is huge for the payment-first approach I care about. Lower fixed costs mean more breathing room every month.

## The Funding Fee Nobody Explains

Here is the part that surprises people. The VA loan has something called a funding fee. It is a one-time cost that helps keep the program going for the next generation of veterans.

A few things worth knowing.

- The fee can usually be rolled into the loan, so you do not pay it out of pocket.
- First-time use has a lower fee than later uses.
- Some veterans are exempt, including many with a service-connected disability rating.

If you are exempt, that is money that stays in your pocket. A lot of veterans do not realize they qualify for the exemption, so it is always worth checking.

## You Can Use It More Than Once

Another myth. People think the VA loan is a one-time thing. It is not.

You can use your VA benefit again and again as long as you meet the requirements. You can even have more than one VA loan at the same time in certain situations, like when you move for a new duty station or a job change here in Nevada.

Your entitlement can be restored after you sell and pay off a prior VA loan. So this benefit follows you through life, not just your first purchase.

## Occupancy and Property Rules

The VA loan is meant for a home you will live in. It is not built for pure investment property. But there is a smart move a lot of veterans miss.

You can buy a multi-unit property with up to four units, live in one, and rent out the others. That means the tenants can help cover your payment while you build equity. For the right buyer in Las Vegas or Henderson, that is a powerful play.

The home also has to meet basic condition standards. The VA wants to make sure you are buying something safe and sound. That protects you, so it is a good thing, not a hurdle.

## Sellers Sometimes Push Back. Do Not Let That Scare You

You may hear that some sellers prefer other offers over VA offers. It happens. Usually it comes from old myths about the process being slow or difficult.

The truth is a VA loan can close on a normal timeline when you work with someone who knows the program. A strong pre-approval and a smooth lender make your offer just as competitive. Do not let an outdated rumor talk you out of the benefit you earned.
