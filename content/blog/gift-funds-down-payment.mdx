A big chunk of my Las Vegas buyers get some help with their down payment from family. That is normal, and it is completely allowed. The part that trips people up is the paperwork, not the money itself.

## Yes, Gift Funds Are Allowed

Conventional, FHA, and VA loans all allow gift funds toward a down payment, closing costs, or reserves, depending on the program and the loan-to-value ratio. This is not a workaround or a loophole. It is a standard part of how home financing works.

## Who Can Give a Gift

Most programs require the gift come from a relative, a domestic partner, or someone with a clearly documented close relationship to the borrower. A random friend writing you a check usually will not qualify without additional documentation explaining the relationship. If you are not sure whether your gift source qualifies, ask before the money moves, not after.

## The Documentation That Actually Matters

This is where deals get delayed. Underwriters need three things.

**A gift letter.** This states the donor's name, relationship to you, the dollar amount, the property address, and confirms in writing that the money is a gift with no expectation of repayment. Your lender provides the template. It takes five minutes to fill out.

**Proof of the donor's ability to give.** This usually means a bank statement showing the funds sitting in the donor's account before the transfer. If the money just appeared in their account, underwriters will want to know where it came from too.

**A paper trail for the transfer itself.** A copy of the check, a wire confirmation, or a screenshot of the transfer. Underwriters need to see money leave the donor's account and land in yours, matching amounts, matching dates.

## The Mistake That Causes the Most Delays

Buyers accept the gift, deposit it, and only tell their lender about it after the fact, sometimes after the bank statements have already been submitted. Any large, unexplained deposit gets flagged. Then you are scrambling for documentation days before closing instead of having it ready from the start.

The fix is simple. Tell your lender before the money moves. We will tell you exactly what paperwork to have ready so the deposit never becomes a question mark.

## Timing Matters Too

Try to have gift funds land in your account well before your final bank statements get pulled for underwriting, ideally at least one full statement cycle earlier. The cleaner the paper trail, the faster this moves.
