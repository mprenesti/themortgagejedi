You did it. You got approved. Your closing date is set. You can almost feel the keys in your hand. And this is exactly the moment where some people accidentally wreck the whole thing.

Here is the truth nobody warns you about. A loan is not final until it funds. Between approval and closing, your file can still be reviewed again. So the things you do in these final weeks matter a lot.

Let me save you from the common mistakes. Here are seven things to never do before closing on a home in Las Vegas.

## 1. Do Not Buy a Car

This is the big one. I see it happen too often. You are excited about the new house, so you go buy a new car to match.

Bad idea. A car payment adds debt to your file. That raises your debt-to-income ratio, and it can push your numbers out of range. A brand new car in the driveway is not worth losing the home. Wait until after you close.

## 2. Do Not Open New Credit Cards

That store card offering you 20 percent off looks tempting. Skip it.

Opening new credit does two things that hurt you right now:

- It adds a hard inquiry, which can nudge your score down.
- It adds a new potential payment to your debt picture.

Do not apply for any new credit until the loan funds. Not a card, not a line of credit, nothing.

## 3. Do Not Make Large Cash Deposits

This one surprises people. Depositing a big pile of cash can actually cause problems.

Underwriters need to trace where your money comes from. A random large deposit raises a question they have to chase down. That slows your loan. If money is coming in, especially a gift, tell me first so we document it the right way. Keep your accounts clean and traceable.

## 4. Do Not Change Jobs

Your income and job history help support your loan. Switching jobs in the middle of the process can shake that foundation.

I am not saying never take a great opportunity. But talk to me before you make a move. Some job changes are fine. Some can pause your loan. A quick conversation protects your closing. Do not just spring it on the process.

## 5. Do Not Miss Any Payments

Keep every bill current. All of them. Your car, your cards, your student loans, everything.

A single late payment during this window can drop your score and raise a red flag. Set reminders. Pay on time. Now is the moment to be the most reliable version of yourself financially. Boring and steady wins here.

## 6. Do Not Move Money Around Without Telling Me

Shuffling money between accounts feels harmless. To an underwriter, it creates a puzzle.

Every dollar you plan to use for closing needs a clear story. When you move funds between accounts right before closing, we have to document all of it. That means more paperwork and more delays. Before you transfer anything, just ask me. One quick message keeps things simple.

## 7. Do Not Co-Sign for Anyone

Your cousin needs a co-signer on a loan. Your heart says yes. Your closing says no.

When you co-sign, that debt can count against you. It shows up as your responsibility. That can change your ratios at the worst possible time. Help your family after you close, not before.

## The One Rule That Covers Them All

If you remember nothing else, remember this. Keep your finances boring until you close.

- No new debt.
- No big purchases.
- No job changes without a heads up.
- No mystery deposits.

Basically, freeze your financial life for a few weeks. Then celebrate all you want once the loan funds and the home is yours.
