## What Homes Actually Cost in the Valley

Prices move, so I will not pin you to a single number. But here is the useful part. Las Vegas and Henderson have a real range of homes, from condos and townhomes to single family houses in newer communities.

A few things that shape your price:

- Location. Closer to the Strip or established neighborhoods can cost more than outlying parts of the valley.
- Property type. A condo with an HOA plays differently in your monthly budget than a standalone home.
- Age and condition. A move-in ready home costs more up front but saves you on repairs.

The trick is not finding the cheapest home. It is finding the home where the total monthly payment fits your life. That is a different question, and it is the right one.

## The Programs Built for First-Time Buyers

You have more options than you probably think. Here are the big ones I use for Las Vegas buyers.

- FHA loans. As little as 3.5 percent down with friendlier credit rules. A great starting point for a lot of first-timers.
- Conventional loans. Some allow 3 percent down for first-time buyers, and can beat FHA on payment depending on your credit.
- Nevada Housing Division help. Programs like Home Is Possible can assist with down payment and closing costs, with income and price limits.
- VA loans. If you served, this can mean zero down and strong terms. Do not leave that benefit on the table.

Which one is right depends on your credit, your income, and your goals. I run the numbers across the options so you can see the real payment side by side.

## Money You Need to Have Ready

Beyond the down payment, plan for a few other costs so nothing surprises you.

- Closing costs. These cover lender, title, and other fees. Assistance programs can help here.
- An earnest money deposit to show the seller you are serious.
- A reserve. Please keep some cash after closing. Life happens, and a Las Vegas summer is hard on an AC unit.

I will always show you the full cash to close, not a lowball estimate. No surprises is a promise, not a slogan.
