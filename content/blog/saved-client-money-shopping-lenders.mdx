I want to tell you about a client I worked with here in Las Vegas. I will keep the details generic to protect their privacy, but the lesson is worth sharing. This is one of the clearest examples I have of why shopping your loan matters, and why a broker can save you real money.

## Where the story started

This client came to me after already getting a quote from their bank. They had been with that bank for years. They had their checking, their savings, and their car loan there. So naturally, when it was time to buy a home, they walked into the branch and asked for a mortgage.

The bank gave them a number. It was not terrible on the surface. But something felt off to them, so they gave me a call before signing anything. I am glad they did.

The first thing I told them was simple. A bank can only offer you the bank's products. That is not a knock on the bank. It is just how it works. They have one menu, and you order off that menu or you leave. As a broker, I have a much bigger kitchen.

## What I actually did

Here is my process, and it is the same for every client.

- I sat down with them and asked about their real goal. Not the biggest house, but a comfortable monthly payment they could live with.
- I gathered their full picture, including income, credit, and how long they planned to stay in the home.
- I took that file to multiple wholesale lenders and asked each of them to compete for the loan.
- I lined up the offers side by side so we could compare the true cost, not just the rate on the front page.

That last part matters. A lot of people only look at the interest rate. But the real cost includes the fees, the points, and how the whole thing shakes out over the years you actually own the home. I break it down so it is easy to see.

## What we found

When I laid the options next to the bank's quote, the difference was clear. One of the wholesale lenders came in with better pricing and lower fees for this client's exact situation. The monthly payment dropped, and the upfront costs came down too.

Over the time this client planned to stay in their Las Vegas home, the savings added up to thousands of dollars. Real money. Money that stays in their pocket for their family, their savings, and their life.

And here is the part I love. The lower payment was not a gimmick. It was not some risky product with a surprise down the road. It was simply a better fit found through honest comparison shopping.

## Why this happens so often

People assume their bank will take care of them because they have a relationship there. I understand that instinct. But loyalty to a single lender can quietly cost you.

When only one lender is competing for your business, they have no reason to sharpen their pencil. When multiple wholesale lenders know they are in a competition, the pricing gets better. That competition works for you, and I am the one who sets it up.

This is also why I focus on the payment first. The bank was happy to approve a bigger number. My client did not need a bigger number. They needed a payment that felt comfortable, and shopping the loan is how we got there.
