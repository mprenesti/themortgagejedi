My phone rings on a Saturday and I pick it up. My phone rings on a Sunday and I pick it up. People are sometimes surprised by that. They expect a voicemail or a text that says they will hear back Monday. But I answer, and I want to tell you why.

## Homes do not wait for business hours

Here is the simple truth. The perfect home in Las Vegas does not care what day it is. Open houses happen on weekends. Offers get written on Saturdays. A seller accepts something on a Sunday afternoon and my client needs an answer fast.

If I only worked banker's hours, my clients would be stuck waiting while other buyers move. That is not fair to them. When you are trying to buy a home, timing can be everything, and I refuse to be the reason a deal slips away.

So when a client calls me on a weekend because they found the one, I am there. We talk it through, we run the numbers, and we make a move. That is the job.

## Buying a home is emotional

A mortgage is not just paperwork. It is one of the biggest decisions of your life. It is your family, your future, and your money all wrapped into one. That comes with real emotions.

I have had clients call me on a weekend not because there was an emergency, but because they were nervous and needed to talk. Maybe they were second guessing themselves. Maybe a family member said something that spooked them. Maybe they just wanted to hear a calm voice say it is going to be okay.

I would rather answer that call and give someone peace of mind than let them stew for two days. A few minutes on the phone can turn a stressful weekend into a good one. That matters to me.

## It is about trust

I did not build my business on flashy ads. I built it on trust and word of mouth here in Las Vegas and Henderson. People send me their friends and family because they know I will treat them right.

Part of treating people right is being reachable. When you know your broker will actually pick up, you feel taken care of. You are not just another file in a stack. You are a person with a real question that deserves a real answer.

That trust is everything. It is why my clients come back years later, and it is why they refer the people they love. I am not going to risk that by being hard to reach when it counts.

## What this says about how I work

Answering the phone on weekends is really about a bigger idea. I am in this with you. I am not just here to close a loan and disappear. I am here to guide you through the whole thing and be there when you need me.

Here is what that looks like in practice.

- I answer when you call, even on a Saturday or Sunday.
- I explain things in plain language, not confusing jargon.
- I focus on a comfortable payment, so you feel good about your decision long after we close.
- I shop your loan across multiple wholesale lenders to find the right fit for you.

None of that works if you cannot reach me. So I keep the phone close.
