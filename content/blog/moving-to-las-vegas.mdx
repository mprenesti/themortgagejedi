Las Vegas continues to draw a steady stream of newcomers relocating from higher cost states, and relocating buyers tend to have a specific set of questions that local buyers do not usually ask. Here is a straightforward guide.

## No State Income Tax Changes Your Real Budget

Nevada has no state income tax, which meaningfully changes your real take-home pay compared to states like California. When budgeting for a home purchase, factor in what your actual net income looks like here, not just a direct comparison of home prices between cities, since your buying power may be stronger than the price difference alone suggests.

## Buying Before You've Moved

If you are purchasing before your job or relocation date is fully finalized, some loan programs are more flexible than others about verifying future employment and income, especially if you already have an accepted offer letter or transfer agreement in hand. Talk to your lender early about your specific relocation timeline, since this affects how your file gets structured.

## Remote Home Shopping Is Common Here

A significant number of my clients purchase homes in this valley without having lived here yet, sometimes touring virtually or making a short trip specifically to view finalists. This is normal in this market, and a good local agent paired with a responsive lender can make a remote purchase feel manageable rather than stressful.

## Property Taxes Are Comparatively Favorable

Nevada's property tax rates run lower than many other states, which can make your total monthly housing cost more favorable than a straight home price comparison would suggest. This is worth understanding clearly if you are relocating from a higher property tax state and trying to accurately compare your new total monthly cost.

## HOA Culture Is More Common Here Than in Some Markets

A large share of housing across the valley, particularly newer construction, comes with HOA dues. If you are relocating from a market where HOAs were less common, factor this into your monthly budget expectations from the start, rather than being surprised by it mid-search.

## Climate and Home Systems Are Different

Homes here are built and maintained differently than homes in colder or more humid climates, HVAC systems work overtime for a large part of the year, and certain maintenance considerations, like desert landscaping and pool upkeep if applicable, are new considerations for buyers relocating from other regions.

## Timing Your Purchase Around Your Move

If your move date is set, work backward from it with your lender to make sure your purchase timeline realistically lines up, factoring in the 25 to 35 day average close time for a typical purchase in this market once you are under contract.
